Terminal‑centric workspace

OTTY is an open‑source workspace that rethinks the terminal itself and turns your shell into one coherent space for development and operating your system.

Built for people who live in the terminal but are tired of switching between editors, SSH clients, dashboards, and a pile of separate tools.

About

Why

Developers spend most of their time in the terminal, but modern terminals remain minimal shells. OTTY builds around the shell experience and adds an IDE‑level workflow on top of the terminal.

Features

Tabs

Keep multiple sessions in one window and switch instantly.

Splits

Work side‑by‑side with split panes for parallel tasks.

Block‑oriented terminal UX

  • Block highlight
  • Semantic copy (prompt / output / command)
