# Define the Product Landing Narrative

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 04

## Question

What single-page narrative, content hierarchy, Product Evidence, and calls to action should explain OTTY's present value as a Terminal-first Workspace, distinguish it from adjacent terminal products, visibly separate Published Release capabilities from `main` previews and Product Vision, and disclose material shell or platform boundaries without becoming a category-site imitation?

## Answer

The Product Landing will be a compact, evidence-led presentation of the latest Published Release. Its narrative is: identify OTTY as an Early Release Terminal-first Workspace, show the four concrete ways it improves current terminal work, and direct the visitor to installation or Documentation. It will not imitate category sites with a long sequence of abstract promises, comparisons, testimonials, or decorative terminal scenes.

### Release truth and boundaries

- Every product claim and piece of Product Evidence must describe the latest Published Release. Features available only on `main` do not appear on the Product Landing.
- Product Vision and roadmap material do not appear on the Product Landing. They must not be visually mixed with Current Capabilities.
- The Hero discloses the currently supported desktop platforms: Linux and macOS.
- Detailed compatibility boundaries, including the enhanced Bash and Zsh Shell Integrations and the behavior of other shells, do not appear as disclaimers on capability cards; the maintainer may cover them in Rolling Documentation.
- The page identifies OTTY as an Early Release; it does not imply product maturity or machine-management and orchestration capabilities that do not exist.

### Page contract

The page has one global header and exactly three content regions: Hero, Capabilities, and Footer. There are no separate About, Why OTTY, workflow story, open-source, roadmap, release, compatibility, testimonial, comparison, newsletter, or closing-CTA sections.

#### Global header

- OTTY logo, linking to the Product Landing.
- Documentation.
- GitHub.
- Download, linking directly to Installation and Downloads.

The header is global navigation, not an additional content region.

#### Hero

The Hero contains:

- a compact Early Release badge;
- the OTTY name;
- the canonical positioning statement: “OTTY is a terminal-first workspace for development and operations across local and remote machines”;
- a primary **Download OTTY** call to action, linking to Installation and Downloads;
- a secondary **Read docs** call to action;
- Linux and macOS availability beside the calls to action;
- one large capture of the actual latest Published Release showing a credible OTTY workspace.

The Hero capture is Product Evidence, not a decorative or reconstructed terminal mockup.

#### Capabilities

The landing uses four capability groups instead of a chronological workflow narrative:

1. **Shape your terminal workspace** — tabs, splits, and the core interactions for arranging terminal sessions.
2. **Work with command blocks** — semantic command and output blocks and the interactions they enable.
3. **Keep project files in reach** — the Explorer working alongside terminal sessions and their current directory.
4. **Launch commands and connections** — Quick Launch for saved commands and SSH connections, presenting OTTY's current SSH Client capability without implying remote-machine management.

Each group contains only a title, one benefit-oriented sentence, and a real screenshot crop or short recording from the latest Published Release. It does not contain a long technical description or compatibility disclaimer; the maintainer may cover those details in Rolling Documentation.

#### Footer

The Footer remains compact and contains:

- OTTY identity;
- Documentation;
- GitHub;
- License;
- copyright and contributor attribution where applicable.

It does not repeat the Download call to action or add a newsletter, large sitemap, or marketing link columns.

### Narrative effect

The page differentiates OTTY through the combination of a Terminal-first Workspace position, restrained release-backed claims, and direct visual proof of real interactions. The visitor should be able to understand the product's present value from the Hero and four capabilities. Installation and Downloads owns installation details; the scope of deeper material in Rolling Documentation remains under maintainer control.
